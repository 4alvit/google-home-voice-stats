# Roadmap: October 2026–October 2027

The project maintains read-only energy reports, local speech and documented Home Assistant/Cast integration. The next year prioritizes dependency and runtime updates, transport regression tests, predictable offline speech output, installation/upgrade guidance, and compatibility with current supported Home Assistant versions. Changes should preserve bounded processing, synthetic tests and household-data privacy.

Near term: complete exact TLS key-boundary coverage for owned clients and speech servers, keep model downloads pinned and hash-verified, and refresh the OpenSSF evidence. Next: improve repeatable device acceptance evidence, warning/coverage reports and accessibility of generated status content. Later: qualify further Cast/HA deployments and add a real maintainer succession arrangement.

Dates are planning horizons, not guarantees. Open issues and pull requests record approved work. Non-goals include public multi-tenant hosting, inverter control/write operations, cloud microphone collection, or claiming that CI proves physical playback and every third-party deployment.
